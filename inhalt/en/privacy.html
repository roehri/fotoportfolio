3. Data collection on this website

Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by Cloudflare and delivered via Cloudflare’s content delivery network. The provider is Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (represented in the EU by Cloudflare Germany GmbH, Rosental 7, 80331 Munich, Germany).

When you visit the website, technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, is routed and processed via Cloudflare’s servers so that the page can be delivered and protected against attacks. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; standard contractual clauses also apply. Further information can be found in Cloudflare’s privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. A data processing agreement exists with the provider.

When the pages are accessed, information is automatically processed via Cloudflare in so-called server log files, which your browser transmits. This is:

  • browser type and browser version
  • operating system used
  • referrer URL
  • host name of the accessing computer
  • time of the server request
  • IP address

We do not merge this data with other data sources. The legal basis for the data processing is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The website operator has a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and security of the website.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not set any cookies itself and does not use analytics, tracking or advertising technologies. If you use the switch at the top right to change between light and dark mode, your choice is stored in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) so that it is retained at your next visit. The language you choose is determined by the page you open. The entry remains solely on your device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. It is only set at your explicit choice and is required for the function you requested (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it at any time in your browser settings.

Contact by e-mail

If you contact us by e-mail, your request including all resulting personal data (name, e-mail address, content of the request) will be stored and processed by us for the purpose of handling your request. We do not pass this data on without your consent. The processing is based on Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR if your request is related to the performance of a contract or is necessary for taking pre-contractual steps, and otherwise on our legitimate interest in the effective handling of enquiries (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data will be deleted as soon as it is no longer required for the purpose of processing and no statutory retention obligations apply.
